## Subscriptions

**What does Mirror cost?** After you set it up, Mirror needs Mirror Premium: yearly, monthly, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy, in your local currency. New subscribers can try the yearly plan free for 7 days.

**How do I cancel?** On your iPhone, open Settings › Apple Account (your name at the top) › Subscriptions › Mirror Premium › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

**How do I get Premium back on a new phone, or after deleting the app?** Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Mirror, and tap **Restore** on the Premium screen. Premium usually comes back by itself, without tapping anything.

**How do I get a refund?** Purchases are made through Apple, and Apple handles refunds. Request one at **[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)**.

**What happens to my rules when my subscription ends?** The next time you open Mirror, your rules switch off and your apps open as normal. Anything you locked in (a strict hold, a lock-in or No Take-Backs) runs to its end first. The apps you picked, your history and your answers are kept.

## Screen Time and blocking

**Why does Mirror need Screen Time access?** It's how iOS lets an app see your usage patterns and hold the apps you choose. Your activity never leaves your phone. See the [Privacy Policy](https://ikiprotich.github.io/Mirror-files/mirror-privacy-policy).

**A blocked app opened anyway, or a block came on a minute late.** Screen Time can take a minute or two to apply a limit after it's reached. If an app stays open well past that, check that Screen Time access is still on in Settings › Screen Time, then open Mirror once.

**Can Mirror block apps on my iPad or Mac?** Not yet. Mirror runs on iPhone, and its rules hold apps on that iPhone. Apple doesn't let apps like Mirror block apps on a Mac at all.

**How do I make Mirror harder to switch off?** Set a Screen Time passcode that someone else knows, and turn on Lock Screen Time Settings. Mirror's Settings explains how.

## Your data

Everything Mirror keeps stays on your iPhone. Settings › Your Data lets you export your history or delete it, and deleting the app removes everything. The [Privacy Policy](https://ikiprotich.github.io/Mirror-files/mirror-privacy-policy) and [Terms of Use](https://ikiprotich.github.io/Mirror-files/mirror-terms-of-use) have the details.
